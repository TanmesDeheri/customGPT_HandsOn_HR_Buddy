## reimbursement?
### To submit a reimbursement at Artscape, follow these simple steps:

1. Submit your expenses via the internal finance portal within **30 days** of incurring the cost.


2. Include valid digital tax invoices or receipts with your submission.


3. Track your claim status (Pending, Approved, or Disbursed) directly through your employee finance dashboard.



Note: Claims go through a two-step verification process (supervisor approval followed by finance audit), and approved amounts are typically credited during the next scheduled payroll cycle.
